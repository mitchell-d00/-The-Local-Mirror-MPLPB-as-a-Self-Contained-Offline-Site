What this page said

The earlier rule required relative links and upward rel declarations but did not require mplpb:status, which made retired and current pages indistinguishable once indexed. That omission is the reason this version was retired.

This page is deliberately unlinked from every active index. It reaches the index through the filesystem audit, not the graph crawl, and is excluded from default retrieval by its status.
